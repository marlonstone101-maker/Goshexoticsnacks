Islandwide Delivery via Knutsford Express 🇯🇲

We offer delivery arrangements for customers across all 14 parishes of Jamaica through Knutsford Express.

Bearer Fee: JMD $1,000 per order. This fee covers the cost of transporting your package from our store to Knutsford Express for shipment.

IMPORTANT NOTICE:

  • The $1,000 bearer fee is charged by our store and is NOT affiliated with Knutsford Express.
  • This fee does NOT include Knutsford Express shipping charges.
  • Customers are responsible for paying Knutsford Express shipping fees separately.
  • Delivery is subject to Knutsford Express service availability at the selected destination.
